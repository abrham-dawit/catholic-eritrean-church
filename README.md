# Catholic Eritrean Church — parish website

This is the church half of an idea I've had for a while: our parishes do a lot by word of mouth, and a simple, warm parish website — Mass times, who we are, our hymns — makes it much easier for people to find us, especially Eritrean families who just moved to a new city and are searching for a church that feels like home.

The other half already exists: the [catholic-mezmur](https://github.com/abrham-dawit/catholic-mezmur) organizer, which this site links to from its Mezmur section.

## What this version has

- **Mass times section** — Sunday, Saturday evening, weekday, and confession blocks.
- **About / our tradition section** — written generically and respectfully about the Eritrean Catholic Church: its Ge'ez-rite heritage, full communion with Rome, fourth-century roots in the Horn of Africa, and what a diaspora parish means to its people. No specific parish's history is invented, because every parish's story is its own.
- **Mezmur section** — explains the hymns and links to the companion Catholic Mezmur Organizer repo.
- **Visit section** — address and contact blocks, plus a welcome note for first-time visitors.
- A small seasonal note on the homepage keyed to the church calendar (Gena, Tsom, the Marian season).

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, no accounts, no servers.

## Placeholders — read before using

This is a **template for a real parish to adopt**, so the parish-specific details are deliberately left as clearly marked placeholders (dashed boxes / bracketed text):

- Parish name and address
- All Mass and confession times
- Parish phone and email

I did **not** put a real parish's name, location, or schedule on this site, because I don't have one parish's confirmed details to publish — and a church website with wrong Mass times is worse than none. When a parish wants to use it, every placeholder is a five-minute find-and-replace.
