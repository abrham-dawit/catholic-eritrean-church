Our Parish & Our Tradition

The Eritrean Catholic Church is one of the Eastern Catholic Churches in full communion with Rome. Our roots run back to some of the oldest Christianity in the world — the faith reached the Horn of Africa in the fourth century, and our liturgy is celebrated in the Alexandrian tradition, in Ge'ez, the ancient liturgical language of our church, with Tigrinya carrying it into daily life.

For Eritreans in the diaspora, the parish is more than a Sunday obligation. It's where children hear the language of their grandparents in the liturgy, where the mezmur they grew up with are sung by a choir that knows them, and where a community that is far from home takes care of its own — the newly arrived, the elderly, families celebrating a baptism, families grieving a loss.

This website is a starting point for our community online: Mass times, our hymns, and a warm welcome to anyone who walks in — Eritrean or not, Catholic or just curious. Come as you are; there's coffee (bun) after Mass, and nobody leaves a stranger.
